### Proj_Ads_Auction:  Building a Second Pricing Auction
* Language: Python
* Codes in this folder are algorithms developed for an online ad auction of a major retailer, in order to model the ad auction and develop an effective algorithm for bidding in a landscape of strategic competitors. It utilizes concept of multi-armed bandit, aims to spend money to explore, learning how likely users are to click on ads, or to exploit, spending on the most promising users to maximize immediate payoffs.
